# Art direction: Knife Cell

Chosen for mission 1 over Salvage Line (A) and Guild Hauler (B). All three generators stay in `tools/blender` for later factions and liveries.

## Shape language
- Single-segment 5 cm chamfers, flat shading
- 22% top taper, so every armor plate leans in like a wedge
- 10% slimmer than the baseline chassis
- No brass. Fasteners are gunmetal and read only up close.

## Palette

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Camo | `#2C2F33`, `#3B3F45`, `#1F2226` (22 cm splinter cells, object space) |
| Plate | `#4A4F55` |
| Frame | `#141618` |
| Emitter / trim | `#C8102E` (player). Lancemates use their callsign color. |

Lance colors (trim, lamps, HUD tags): Gideon `#FF4A5A`, Anvil `#FFB347`, Kestrel `#5FD8FF`, Ratchet `#8DFFB0`.

## World: Harrow Basin at dusk
- Low orange sun (about 15° elevation), blue-violet sky, warm fog
- Sand `#9E7350` to `#B88C61`; rock `#5C4336` to `#80604A` with horizontal strata on cliffs
- Ground detail comes from shader normals (wind ripples, grit, rock chips) plus scattered pebbles, scrub, boulders and hoodoos
- Keep the patrol route in a readable sandy wash; walls and mesas frame it

## Readability rules
- Red belongs to threats: Sable drone eyes, sabots, HUD warnings. The player's red trim is the one exception, and it only shows on the player's own mech.
- The HUD is bone white (`#E9E4D6`). Cyan marks navigation and tutorial hints.
- Sabots must be the brightest thing on screen: a white-hot core, a red shell, a light and a trail.
